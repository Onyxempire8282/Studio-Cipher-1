5) Notable inconsistencies
- claim_cipher_app/index.html has no linked stylesheet.
- claim_cipher_app/reset-password.html uses paths prefixed with "claim_cipher_app/" while other pages use "styles/..."; likely a broken relative path.
- Base/global rules are spread across multiple files (universal-system.css, cipher-core.css, main.css, glassmorphism-universal.css, universal-header-fixes.css), which increases cascade ambiguity and raises risk of regression when modifying global typography or resets.
